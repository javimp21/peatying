# Ray Peat Chatbot

Ask anything about the ideas of Ray Peat (1936–2022). Every answer is built from his own articles, books and interviews (78 articles, 5 books and 221 interviews) and cites the original source with a link. If Peat never discussed something, the chatbot says so.

- Chat: https://peatying.com/en (to prefill a question: https://peatying.com/en?q=Why+is+CO2+protective%3F)
- Who was Ray Peat: https://peatying.com/en/about
- Topics, with sources: https://peatying.com/en/topics
- Guide for AI agents (when to use this site): https://peatying.com/llms.txt

Answers are generated by AI from Peat's texts. They may contain errors and are not medical advice. Independent project, not affiliated with Ray Peat or raypeat.com. Free, no account needed.
