Privacy

This site is a personal, non-profit project. It keeps the minimum it needs to work and to know whether it is used. There are no cookies, no ads and no third-party tracking tools.

Who is responsible

Francisco Javier Martínez Pérez. Contact: fjaviermap [at] gmail.com or LinkedIn.

What is kept

  • Your questions and the answers. They let the chat remember the conversation and help improve the service. They are not linked to your name or to an account (there are no accounts). Before they are stored, data such as ID numbers, dates of birth, medical record numbers, emails or phone numbers are automatically blanked out, for example if you paste lab results. It is an automatic filter: better not to write your name or other personal data.
  • Your history, in your browser. So you can go back to your conversations from “History”, they are saved in your own browser's storage (not in an account). They can only be seen from that browser, and you can rename or delete them at any time from the same window.
  • Documents you attach (lab results, a report…). The file is not kept: it is sent to Anthropic to extract its text and then discarded. That text, with personal data blanked out as in the questions, is stored with the conversation so the chat can use it in your next questions, and is deleted with it. Even so, better not to upload documents with your name or ID number.
  • Answers you share. If you press “Share”, a public copy of that question and its answer (with nothing that identifies you) is stored with its own link, so whoever receives it can see it. That copy does not expire after 12 months; if you want it deleted, write to me with the link.
  • Votes 👍/👎 on each answer.
  • Visits. To count visitors, an anonymous fingerprint that changes every day is kept (it can't tell who you are or follow you from one day to the next), the site you came from (just the domain, for example “google.com”) and your approximate country and city, worked out from your IP on our own server. The IP is not stored.

How long it is kept

Conversations are deleted automatically after 12 months. Visit data, which is anonymous, after 24 months. Your history in the browser stays there until you delete it.

Who it is shared with

To answer, your question is sent to Anthropic (the Claude AI model), and the search over Peat's texts goes through Voyage AI. Both companies are in the United States. The site is hosted on Amazon Web Services, in Ireland. Nothing is sold or given to anyone else.

Legal basis

Legitimate interest in the service working, not being abused and being improved (article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).

Your rights

You can ask for a conversation to be deleted, or exercise your rights of access, rectification, objection or erasure, by writing to the contact above. As there are no accounts, include the text of one of your questions and the day so it can be found. If you think your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.
