Ray Peat on coconut oil

For Ray Peat, coconut oil was a very stable saturated fat that, unlike polyunsaturated vegetable oils, didn't slow metabolism or promote obesity 12. He held that replacing unsaturated fats with coconut supported thyroid function and reduced metabolic stress 34.

An oil that slims instead of fattening

Peat came to coconut in the 1970s through a study of rats fed fifteen different diets. According to his account, at the end of their lives the leanest were the ones eating coconut oil, on a high- or low-fat diet, and the fattest were those eating only polyunsaturated fat, even on low-fat diets 56. From this he concluded that what mattered wasn't the amount of fat but its degree of unsaturation 5.

He also recalled that in the 1940s farmers tried cheap coconut oil to fatten their animals and got lean, active, hungry animals 3. That is why he considered it the least fattening of all oils 1.

Why coconut is so stable

Peat stressed that coconut oil kept for a year at room temperature showed no signs of going rancid, even though it contains a small percentage of unsaturated fats, which one would expect to oxidise 32. His hypothesis was that the abundance of saturated fat keeps the unsaturated molecules apart, so they can't set off chains of destructive reactions 3.

According to him, this acts as an antioxidant function inside the body too, and he pointed out that coconut reduces the need for vitamin E 32. He also explained that plants in very hot regions make saturated fats because at those temperatures unsaturated ones would oxidise at once, and he considered this a biological adaptation 7.

Thyroid, blood sugar and energy

He held that when the body draws on stored unsaturated fats, the whole thyroid system suffers in proportion to their unsaturation: less hormone is produced, it is transported less well and it enters the mitochondria and nucleus less well 4. He added that unsaturated oils inhibit the proteolytic enzyme that forms thyroid hormone, and that coconut would prevent that inhibiting effect 3.

According to Peat, some medium-chain saturated fatty acids curb fat formation in the liver, which leaves sugar available to be used or stored as glycogen 3. For him, this reduced the need for adrenal hormones, and people who used coconut regularly noticed that they could go hours without eating and without symptoms of hypoglycaemia 3.

Peat's personal experience with coconut

Peat told how, when he added a tablespoon of coconut oil to his evening meal, he noticed for one or two hours a faster heart rate, heavier breathing and pink skin 843. On one occasion he measured his pulse at about 98 beats 3. After several weeks, that reaction faded 83.

He said he lost weight even though he was eating more calories, and that he levelled off at a weight about ten or twelve pounds lower 84. He interpreted the initial surges as the body compensating for an antithyroid substance, and then adjusting as the inhibition decreased 3.

Ageing, skin and brain

Peat linked ageing, especially of the brain, to lipid peroxidation 3. He cited work according to which the age pigment forms in proportion to the ratio of unsaturated to saturated fats, and stated that the amount of unsaturated fat in the diet affects how quickly wrinkled skin appears 3. Ultraviolet light, according to him, causes peroxidation in unsaturated fats and not in saturated ones 3.

He mentioned an experiment from around 1978 in which the offspring of mice fed coconut showed better brain development than those of mice fed unsaturated oil 3. He noted that this could be due to coconut's support of the thyroid, but added that in young rats soy oil was incorporated into brain cells, which turned out structurally abnormal 3.

Frequently asked questions

Is coconut oil fattening according to Ray Peat?
For Peat it was the least fattening of oils 1. He recalled that farmers who used it to fatten animals got lean ones, and that in rat studies the leanest were those fed coconut 531.
Why doesn't coconut oil go rancid?
Peat pointed out that coconut kept for a year at room temperature showed no deterioration 32. His explanation was that the large proportion of saturated fat keeps the unsaturated molecules apart, so they can't trigger chain reactions of oxidation 3.
What effect does coconut oil have on the thyroid according to Peat?
He held that coconut supports thyroid function because it dilutes and displaces unsaturated fats, which in his view inhibit the production, transport and action of thyroid hormone 43. He also linked it to a slight drop in cholesterol, something he saw as expected when thyroid function rises 1.
What other fats did Ray Peat consider safe?
In an interview he named as safe fats butter, cocoa butter (mostly stearic acid), coconut oil, palm kernel oil, beef and lamb fat, and olive oil 9. By contrast, for him the commonly used polyunsaturated fats were harmful 10.
