Ray Peat on the gut and endotoxin

According to Peat, endotoxin is a fragment of the coat of intestinal bacteria that, when absorbed in excess, disrupts the cells' energy production and triggers inflammation 12. For him, chronic constipation, the wrong kind of fibre and stress favour that absorption, with consequences that go far beyond the bowel 341.

What endotoxin is according to Peat

Peat explained that endotoxin is part of the structure of the bacterial coat: a starch-like molecule with fatty acids attached. Bacteria release it when they multiply or are under stress 2.

According to him, organisms have lived with it for so long that it works as a basic signal to defend against the environment. That is why it readily triggers substances such as serotonin and nitric oxide 2. It also has an irritating effect on the lining of the intestine and on blood vessels when endotoxin manages to get out of the bowel 2.

Cell energy and the intestinal barrier

For Peat, in the presence of endotoxin the cells lining the intestine lose their ability to produce energy by respiration, and nitric oxide would probably be the main mediator of that effect 1. The cell shifts from respiration to glycolysis, that is, from producing carbon dioxide to producing lactic acid, and gives up its specialised function to devote itself to defensive and inflammatory processes 1.

He held that this change alters the properties of the cytoplasm, which tends to swell and let in substances that would not normally pass 1. He rejected the idea that permeability is explained only by gaps between cells: in his view, an inflamed or fatigued cell weakens its barrier function, which is why permeability varies with the substance 1.

Constipation, inflammation and vicious circles

According to Peat, constipation associated with high adrenaline prolongs the absorption of bacterial endotoxin, while acute diarrhoea quickly sweeps the contents away and usually reduces it. That is why he considered occasional diarrhoea somewhat therapeutic 4. He also recalled that chronic constipation has been associated with cancer and other diseases for centuries 4.

For him, ageing and stress increase inflammatory mediators that reduce the intestinal barrier, let more bacterial toxins into the blood and create vicious circles of more inflammation and more permeability 1. He described an intestinal wall that can swell until it thickens a lot, narrows the lumen and loses its ability to contract, so that neither a stimulant nor bulky fibre would help in that case 1. He argued that anti-inflammatories, such as aspirin or antihistamines, can restore smooth muscle contraction 1. He also pointed out that low thyroid function and other endocrine problems are rarely considered in chronic constipation 1.

Fibre, foods and bacteria

Peat held that poorly digestible foods that reach the bowel with nutritional value feed bacteria and favour their overgrowth. Fruit, by contrast, is digested and absorbed quickly, so little of it reaches the bacterial zone 3. He criticised the promotion of fibre and indigestible starches for gut health: he cited Australian studies in which people who ate a lot of oat bran seemed to have a higher risk of bowel cancer 31.

According to him, some fibres cause such intense bacterial growth that toxins other than endotoxin are produced, such as proteins and fats modified from undigested food 3. Even so, he thought the right kind of fibre can benefit several bowel problems 1. As an example he mentioned raw carrot, which according to him reduces endotoxin absorption and contains natural antibiotics 56.

Endotoxin, hormones and disease

For Peat, endotoxin raises blood estrogen, and chronic constipation or anxiety can increase the liver's exposure to it 7. He added that an excess of estrogen relative to progesterone reduces bowel motility, probably by increasing nitric oxide 1. According to him, penicillin, probably by reducing endotoxin production, lowers estrogen and cortisone and raises progesterone, and raw carrots would achieve a similar effect 7.

He also linked endotoxin to other problems. He said he had seen cases of people with chronic constipation who suffered strokes, aneurysms and abnormal clotting, and stated that brain haemorrhages are promoted by endotoxin and the inflammation it causes 4. On cancer, he argued that reducing endotoxin absorption would be a minimally reasonable approach, because endotoxaemia can produce aerobic glycolysis in a healthy person 5. He also noted that Parkinson's disease often begins with constipation 8.

Gut bacteria and antibiotics

Peat explained that the more harmless bacteria in the colon compete with the more irritating ones, and that many yogurt-type probiotics do not survive healthy digestion. In his judgement, something is needed that combines harmless bacteria with a competitive antibiotic, and he gave products based on Bacillus subtilis as an example 9. He added that certain fungi and bacteria are both probiotic and antibiotic, and that within a few hours you can tell whether an antibiotic supplement helps 10.

According to him, part of the benefit of antibiotics comes from the reduced endotoxin stress when gut bacteria are suppressed 6. He warned that these drugs eliminate the bacteria that produce vitamin K, so it is worth getting it from the diet 611. He stressed, however, that there is no single pathogenic substance in the gut: everything depends on the bacteria, the food and how the bowel responds 8.

Frequently asked questions

What is endotoxin according to Ray Peat?

Why did Peat link constipation to endotoxin?
According to him, constipation with high adrenaline prolongs the absorption of bacterial endotoxin 4. He also thought inflammation of the intestinal wall weakens the barrier and lets more toxins into the blood 1.
Was Peat in favour of eating a lot of fibre?
Not in general. He held that fibre and indigestible starches can feed excessive bacterial growth and produce toxins, and he cited oat bran as an example associated with higher bowel cancer risk 31. He did consider, however, that the right kind of fibre, such as raw carrot, can be beneficial 51.
What role did Peat give to raw carrot?
According to Peat, a grated carrot with salt and vinegar can greatly improve gut health and reduce endotoxin absorption 5. He added that it contains natural antibiotics and can lower estrogen in a way similar to penicillin 76.
