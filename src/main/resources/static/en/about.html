Who was Ray Peat?

Raymond Peat (1936–2022) was an American biologist with a PhD from the University of Oregon, specialising in physiology. He spent decades researching hormones and metabolism, and taught at several universities, including in Mexico. He wrote dozens of articles that are still read and discussed in forums and on social media.

His ideas, in a nutshell

For Peat, health depends above all on how much energy your cells produce. His best-known positions follow from that: look after your thyroid, don't fear the sugar in fruit, distrust polyunsaturated vegetable oils, and see stress as the body's great source of wear. Many of them go against today's medical consensus.
